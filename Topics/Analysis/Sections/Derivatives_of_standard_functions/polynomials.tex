\subsubsection{Polynomials}
\subsubsubsection{Degree-1 polynomials}
Let a function \(f : \R \to \R\) be defined as \(f(x) = x\). For the derivative for a fixed point \(a \in \R\), we have from equation (1.2.5.1) that
\begin{equation}
    f'(a) = \lim_{x \to a} \frac{f(x) - f(a)}{x - a} = \lim_{x \to a} \frac{x - a}{x - a} = \lim_{x \to a} \frac{1}{1} = 1
\end{equation}
Redefine \(f\) to the general form \(f(x) = mx + b\). We then have
\begin{equation}
    f'(a) = \lim_{x \to a} \frac{f(x) - f(a)}{x - a} = \lim_{x \to a} \frac{mx + b - ma - b}{x - a} = \lim_{x \to a} \frac{m(x - a)}{x - a} = \lim_{x\to a} \frac{m}{1} = m
\end{equation}
